# Visual prompts — MARKOVMADE: RECODE 7.0.0

Art direction: original cinematic editorial urban realism; adult characters;
obsidian, platinum, muted gold and controlled teal; rain, glass and directional
light; quiet tension; natural anatomy; no third-party logos, branded clothing,
artist imitation or text baked into images.

## Desktop hero

Create a premium cinematic key visual for MARKOVMADE: RECODE — Life RPG at
2400×1500. Night-time Meridian after rain. One adult man in a restrained black
coat stands on the right, seen from behind in three-quarter profile, facing a
glass-and-concrete city threshold. Leave the entire left half dark and quiet
for a large editorial headline and product UI. Black architecture, wet
reflections, muted platinum light, one restrained gold source, slight teal
signals, subtle film grain, realistic hands and anatomy, no weapons, no
superhero pose, no logos, no readable text, no UI.

## Mobile hero

Create a portrait 1086×1448 companion to the desktop key visual. Preserve the
same adult man, coat, rain-soaked Meridian and restrained obsidian/platinum/gold
palette. Place the subject in the lower-right third, keep the upper-left and
center-left calm enough for mobile headline and controls, retain depth at narrow
crop, no baked text, no logos, no imitation of an existing franchise.

## Meridian cast

Create a 1586×992 cinematic ensemble image with exactly five distinct adults in
Meridian during a consequential conversation: diverse faces, ages roughly
28–50, believable modern dark clothing, no uniforms or logos. Frame them as
people with competing motives rather than quest dispensers. Wet glass city
interior, directional platinum light, restrained gold edge light, controlled
teal reflections, natural anatomy and hands, no weapons, no text. Keep central
faces readable and edges crop-safe.

## Production derivatives

Source PNGs live in `art_source/generated/`. Runtime AVIF/WebP/PNG fallbacks
live in `web_app/public/art/key/`. Derivatives are local conversions of the
source PNGs. Any commercial launch still requires owner trademark/likeness and
platform-policy review.
